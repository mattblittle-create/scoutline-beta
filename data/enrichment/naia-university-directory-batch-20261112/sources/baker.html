Athletic Programs

Where Champions Rise

At Baker University, athletics are more than a tradition—they’re a defining part of the Wildcat experience. Our student-athletes compete with heart, strive for excellence, and build lifelong skills in leadership, teamwork, and resilience. Whether you’re here to cheer, compete, or get involved, this is your hub for everything Wildcat athletics.

Explore the Official Athletics Site

Why Compete at Baker

Student-athletes choose Baker for strong coaching, personalized academic support, upgraded facilities, and a tradition that helps them compete at the highest levels of NAIA athletics. 

A Proven Record of Athletic Success 

  • Compete in the Heart of America Athletic Conference and the NAIA
  • Twenty-seven varsity programs
  • Ten individual national champions since 2012
  • Winningest football program in NAIA history (647+ wins)
  • Historic coaching lineage including Phog Allen
  • Alumni success stories including NFL coach Mike McCarthy 

Academic and Career Strength 

  • Twelve to one student-to-faculty ratio
  • One hundred percent of CAS students receive financial aid
  • Number one in Kansas for graduate earnings and national top-tier ROI

Facilities and Training Environment

High-quality facilities designed to support year-round performance. 

Baker’s athletic footprint continues to expand through the Baker Builds Champions initiative, bringing modern training spaces and competitive facilities to campus. 

Baseball and Softball Complex Enhancements 

Significant upgrades to Sauder Field (baseball) and Cavaness Field (softball) include: 

  • New full-field turf
  • New dugouts
  • Stadium lighting
  • Enhanced stands and fan experience
  • A shared indoor hitting facility for year-round training 

Soccer-Specific Field 

Baker soccer competes on a dedicated outdoor turf soccer field located within the upgraded athletics complex.  

Features include: 

  • Professional-grade turf
  • Energy-efficient lighting
  • Improved run-off and drainage
  • Enhanced fan viewing areas 

Track and Field Enhancements 

  • Relocated and improved throwing areas
  • Track resurfacing
  • Strengthened field-event training zones 

Indoor Training Capacity 

New indoor training spaces, including the turf-hitting facility and expanded practice areas, support year-round conditioning for multiple sports. 

Existing Facilities 

  • Liston Stadium (football, track and field)
  • Collins Center (basketball, volleyball, wrestling, cheer, dance)
  • Strength and conditioning facilities
  • Athletic training and rehab environments
