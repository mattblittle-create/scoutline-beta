BASEBALL CAMPS

OKWU Eagles Baseball Camps offer your player the opportunity to improve their fundamental baseball skills. They will be taught by Eagles Coaching Staff and Players. The Eagles Baseball team has been nationally ranked for the past several years, competing in National Tournaments.

BASKETBALL CAMPS

OKWU Eagles Basketball Camps offer your player several different opportunities to learn or improve their basketball skills. Boy & Girls Camps for a variety of ages will be offered along with a camp focused just for junior high and a summer basketball league.

SOCCER CAMPS

OKWU Eagles Soccer Camps offer your player a soccer experience from an international coaching staff. We tailor the camp curriculum to suit boys and girls of all ages abilities. Along with developing fundamental skills, players will get to compete in competitions for prizes.

Team camps are also offered this summer to help teams get better over the summer together.
