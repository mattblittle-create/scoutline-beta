Cavalier Camps

Montreat College Athletics offers sports camps throughout the summer for children of all ages, as well as prospective student-athletes. Camps are led by Montreat’s coaches, their staff, and current student-athletes. Please review the list below to learn more about all upcoming Cavalier Camps.

Nike Lacrosse Camp

This camp mixes elite lacrosse instruction with an incredible summer experience.  Young players will have a fantastic opportunity to learn from amazing coaches and have a blast in the process!

Lacrosse Camp

Montreat Running Camp

Participants will get in shape, grow in faith, and conquer Lookout Mountain. In addition to running training and instruction from our expert coaches, you’ll also enjoy swimming, Bible studies, worship, and our high ropes courses for a week you’ll never forget!

Running Camp

Nike Softball Camp

Learn applicable skills for your game and utilize them to become a better player! Get the full softball experience with drills covering fielding, hitting, and game time situations.

Softball Camp

Nike Baseball Camp

Participants will receive professional instruction from Montreat College coaches and athletes. Each day will be filled with baseball drills that cover hitting, fielding, base running, and team play to help each camper take their game to the next level!

Baseball Camp

Women’s Soccer Camp

See how a relationship-based approach to the game of soccer facilitates an atmosphere of freedom that empowers you to discover the soccer player you were made to be.
