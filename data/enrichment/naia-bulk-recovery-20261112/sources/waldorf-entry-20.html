Waldorf Athletics - The RIGHT Way to Play!

When you’re part of Waldorf Athletics, you become part of something greater than yourself. Across our 20+ athletic teams, our student-athletes exemplify values that have shaped generations of Waldorf graduates:

  • Integrity 
  • Responsibility 
  • Respect 
  • Sportsmanship 
  • Servant Leadership 

These core values define who we are—on the field, on the court, and beyond. Our athletes don't just compete; they lead, serve, and strive for excellence in every aspect of life. At Waldorf University, you’ll have the opportunity to pursue all your passions, whether for sports, music, theatre, leadership, or more, while gaining a top-tier education and building lifelong relationships.

We’re committed to helping student-athletes balance academic achievement with athletic success. Through this balance, our students develop critical life skills such as leadership, teamwork and time management—traits that are highly sought after in today’s competitive job market.

Waldorf Athletics is a proud member of the Great Plains Athletic Conference (GPAC) and participates in other competitive coed sports. Our athletes embody resilience, dedication and pride, inspiring our entire community—from students to alumni.

Ready to take the next step?
Visit our campus and explore what it means to be part of a program that challenges you to grow in every way. Schedule your visit today and see how Waldorf University can help you become the best version of yourself.
