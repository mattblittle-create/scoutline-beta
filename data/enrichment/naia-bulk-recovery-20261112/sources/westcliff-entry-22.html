Westcliff University Athletics

Since joining the NAIA and Cal Pac in 2021, Westcliff Athletics has won six national championships, 25 conference championships and five individual championships for a total of 31 athletic championships. Individually, Westcliff has produced 23 NAIA All Americans.

Academically, Westcliff Athletics has produced 118 NAIA scholar athletes and 36 California Pacific Conference All Academic performers.

Our athletic department is guided by the 5Cs; we strive to find great athletes with superior CHARACTER, who can establish an exceptional CULTURE, by making a tremendous impact in the COMMUNITY, by being fully COMMITTED to excellence, which will translate into CHAMPIONSHIPS! Our 5Cs are the epitome of who we are and what we do on and off the field of play.

Our athletes work hard in the classroom and seek to be students first, representing Westcliff University as exemplary student-athletes.  Our teams compete as true Warriors and battle wholeheartedly against all levels of competition. We recruit athletes from near and far and represent countries from all around the world. Check our site for the team’s schedules and come cheer on your Westcliff Warriors!
