Legacy Starts Here

USAO Homecoming 2026

This November, USAO is bringing generations of Drovers together in a new way as Homecoming 2026: Legacy Starts Here welcomes alumni back to campus alongside the university’s first-ever Family Weekend. On November 6–7, 2026, alumni, students, families, faculty, staff and friends are invited to experience USAO together, celebrating the traditions and relationships that have shaped our university while making new memories with the students and families who are part of its story today.
