NHCLC OFFICIAL SCHOOL OF CHOICE
Life Pacific University is proud to be named the official School of Choice for the National Hispanic Christian Leadership Conference. Through this five-year partnership, LPU is expanding access to Christ-centered undergraduate, graduate, seminary, and bilingual education for pastors, ministry leaders, students, and families across the NHCLC network. Together, we are helping equip the next generation of leaders to serve with faith, excellence, and purpose.

LPU is committed to creating an inclusive environment.

We are a Christian university fully committed to honoring and celebrating the Imago Dei – the image of God (Genesis 1:26) in each student, faculty, and staff member. We celebrate diversity as an expression of God’s creativity and aim to build a diverse, Christ centered community that fully engages students in the transformative educational process that prepares them to be leaders in the Church, the workplace, and the world.
