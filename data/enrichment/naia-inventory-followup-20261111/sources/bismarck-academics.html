Academics

Degrees DONE RIGHT.

At Bismarck State College, we believe education is the key to unlocking your full potential, whether you're a recent high school graduate eager to kick-start your career or a seasoned professional looking to enhance your skills. With a commitment to excellence and innovation, we offer a wide range of programs that will help you reach your personal educational and career goals.

Choose a variety of in-demand 2-year technical degrees or short-term certificates designed with a polytechnic focus, keeping hands-on learning in mind. Our programs are crafted to meet the demands of ever-evolving industries, ensuring you stay at the forefront of your field. Whether your passion is in IT, healthcare or engineering, we have the tools and expertise to help you succeed.

Whether you want to continue your education with a bachelor’s degree, BSC offers four-year degrees on campus or 2-year transfer degrees that allow you to start here and finish anywhere. Whether your heart is set on business, the arts, the sciences, or beyond, our dedicated faculty and comprehensive curriculum will empower you to excel in your chosen path.
We understand that your journey is unique, and that's why we offer many supportive services tailored to your individual needs. From academic advising to career counseling, we're here to ensure you have the tools and resources necessary to succeed in your classes and reach your destination.

And the best part? Achieving your dreams at BSC won't break the bank. We're committed to making higher education accessible to everyone by offering affordable tuition and over $1 million in scholarships. Your future is an investment, and at BSC, we're dedicated to delivering exceptional value for your hard-earned money.

At Bismarck State College, you will get the right skills to land the right career and tuition that will get you there.

BSC–College DONE RIGHT.
