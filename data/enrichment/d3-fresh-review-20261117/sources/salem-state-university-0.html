    ATHLETES OF THE WEEK
    Krysta Zamejtis

    Krysta Zamejtis (Jr., Danvers, Mass.) -- In last week's 2-0 regular-season finale win over Nichols, Zamejtis potted the eventual game-winner less than 10 minutes (06:34) in. With the win, Salem secured its spot in the MASCAC Playoffs and will face Fitchburg State on Wednesday, November 4 at 6:30 pm. 

    Iago De Sousa-Vieira

    Iago De Sousa-Vieira (Gr., Medford, Mass.) -- De Sousa-Vieira had a strong week for the Vikings, helping the squad go 2-0-0 with wins over Dean College and Bridgewater State. De Sousa-Vieira totaled two goals and two assists. In the 6-0 win over Dean, he scored the team's first goal and assisted on the squad's second. In the 2-0 win at Bridgewater, the graduate forward scored the team's second goal and assisted on the eventual game-winner. He is now tied for the conference lead in goals (six) while also ranking second in points (17). Thanks to his strong play last week, he earned MASCAC Co-Offensive Player of the Week honors.

    Sabina Kamanda

    Sabina Kamanda (Sr., Dracut, Mass.) -- Kamanda helped the Vikings go 2-0 this week, winning at home on senior day over the Trailblazers of MCLA. She dished out a total of 22.5 points for the Vikings on the day. Kamanda would post a career high in digs on the day over the Trailblazers with 22, along with a career high in kills with 21 on the day. Kamanda would have a strong week for the Viking’s to help lead the Viking’s to a 1-0 week and a key conference win over the Trailblazers.

    Izzy Mosley

    Izzy Mosley (Sr., Middleborough, Mass.) -- Last week, the senior goalie tallied 39 saves in 60:00 of action against Worcester State and Westfield State. That same week, Mosley also became the program's all-time saves leader, surpassing the previous mark of 681 held by Carolyn Woods'16. Mosley now has 721 career saves and leads the country in saves per game (21.14). 

    Celia Carbone

    Celia Carbone (Sr., Lynnfield, Mass.) -- Carbone helped the Vikings go 2-1 last week, winning at Fitchburg and Elms. She dished out a total of 79 assists (26.3 APG) and became the program’s all-time assists leader in the win at Fitchburg State (1,527 and counting). Carbone also posted 11 service aces (3.6 per game) and surpassed the 100-career service ace mark (106 and counting) in the same Fitchburg game. 

    Olivia Henke

    Olivia Henke (Sr., Bay Shore, N.Y.) -- Henke played a key role in Salem's 1-0-1 week, netting the game-winner in a 1-0 win over Bridgewater State. The victory over the Bears marked the squad's first win of the season and its first win against Bridgewater since 2014. In the 0-0 draw against UMaine Farmington, Henke was all over the field, ensuring the Beavers didn't get any good looks as Salem's defense allowed six shots. She also chipped in offensively with three shots, with two on net. 

    Ryann Tripp

    Ryann Tripp (Sr., Wareham, Mass.) -- Team captain Ryann Tripp has been an anchor for the women's tennis team this season. Not just in last week's match versus UMass Boston, but in every game and practice, her leadership has been "invaluable," said Head Coach Suzanne Lazzaro. 

    Liam Hyatt

    Liam Hyatt (Sr., D, Endwell, N.Y.) -- Last week, after going 2-0-0 against MIT and Westfield State, Hyatt had a goal and an assist in a 4-1 win over Westfield State last Saturday. He was also a key member of the Salem defense that has yet to allow a goal this year.

    Jake Boucher

    Jake Boucher (Gr., P/INF, Wallingford, Conn.) -- Boucher was named MASCAC Pitcher of the Week after throwing a two-hit shutout in his first career start, retiring 22-of-24 batters with seven strikeouts and no walks in the Vikings’ 7-0 victory over MCLA. He struck out five straight at one point to improve to 3-1 on the year.

    Fowler Gianna

    Gianna Fowler (Fr., C, East Haven, Conn.) -- Fowler batted .333 (3-for-9) with three home runs, six RBI and three runs scored for Salem State (1-2) this week. The freshman went 3-for-4 with three home runs, six RBI and three runs in a 12-5 victory over MCLA.

    Matt Tramonte

    Matt Tramonte (Fr., Tewksbury, Mass.) -- Tramonte earned MASCAC Athlete and Rookie of the Week after leading Salem State at the Dr. Tim Brown Invitational at Ballston Spa Country Club, tying for 29th overall with a two-day 147, carding an even par-71 on day one, and a +5, 76 on day two.

    Allison Frost

    Allison Frost (Jr., OF, Auburn, Mass.) -- Frost was named MASCAC Softball Player of the Week after batting .400 (4-10) with two runs, two RBI, four walks and four steals in a 3-1 for the Vikings. Went 1-for-2 with three walks, a run and RBI in a 13-8 win over St. Joe’s. Added two hits in five at-bats with a walk, run and RBI in a doubleheader sweep over Southern Maine.

    Ethan Stepputtis

    Ethan Stepputtis (Fr., OF, Prospect, Conn.) -- Stepputtis earned MASCAC Rookie of the Week honors after batting .600 (3-for-5) with two runs, a stolen base and an RBI in the Vikings’ 13-8 win over Westfield State in their lone game of the week.

    Alanis Toledano

    Alanis Toledano (So., INF/P, Boston, Mass.) -- Toledano was named MASCAC Pitcher of the Week after firing a complete game one-hitter for Salem State, with seven strikeouts and five walks in a 4-0 shutout over Bridgewater State. Also went 4-for-6, batting .667, with a home run, two RBI and a run scored.

    Nolan Currier

    Nolan Currier (So., 1B, Maynard, Mass.) -- Currier batted .667 (6-13) with a double, triple, home run and three RBI and four runs scored in a 1-3 week for the Vikings. He was 2-for-3 with a triple and three runs scored in a 10-8 victory over Colby Sawyer. Went 3-for-5 with a home run two RBI and a run scored in a 5-4 extra-innings setback to Bridgewater State.
