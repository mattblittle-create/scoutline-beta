    Cobber Baseball to Host Fall Prospect Camp on Sept. 13

    The Concordia College baseball program will host its Fall Prospect Camp on Sunday, Sept. 13, 2026, beginning at 11 a.m. at Bucky Burgau Baseball Field.

    The Concordia College baseball program will host its Fall Prospect Camp on Sunday, Sept. 13, 2026, beginning at 11 a.m. at Bucky Burgau Baseball Field.

    The camp is open to players in the graduating classes of 2027 and 2028 and is designed to give prospective student-athletes the opportunity to improve their skills, compete in a college-style setting, and showcase their abilities in front of the Cobber coaching staff.

    Throughout the camp, players will work directly with the Concordia baseball coaching staff through a combination of positional instruction, skill development, showcase activities, and game situations. The day will provide athletes with the chance to learn more about the Cobber baseball program, experience a college-level practice environment, and gain valuable insight into the recruiting process.

    Prospective student-athletes and their families are also encouraged to visit campus for Cobber Fest on Saturday, Sept. 12. The annual campus celebration begins at 3 p.m. and provides an opportunity to experience the Concordia community before attending the prospect camp the following day.

    The camp will be led by Head Coach Anthony Renz, who became the 20th head coach in program history in 2023 after spending five seasons as an assistant coach with the Cobbers from 2019-23. Along with his role at Concordia, Renz has served as the hitting and bench coach for the Fargo-Moorhead RedHawks since 2018, helping the team earn an American Association championship.
