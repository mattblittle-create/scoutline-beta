St. Lawrence Baseball Prospect ID Clinic

St. Lawrence Baseball Prospect ID Clinic
One-Day Prospect Clinic - Open to any High School Athletes - September 6, 2026
Tom Fay Field
St. Lawrence University

 

 
SCHEDULE:
8:00-8:15 a.m. - Registration
8:30 a.m. - Warm Up
9:00 a.m. - Defensive Workout/Batting Practice
9:45 a.m. - Pitcher Check-In
11:00 a.m. - Scrimmage
1:00 p.m. - Lunch (Bring your own or $15 dollars) and Campus Tours with Admissions (optional)
(Participants with any severe food allergies must bring their own bagged lunch clearly marked with their name.)

 
CAMP SPECIFICS:
- Position players will work out at their positions, take batting practice, and get tested for exit velocity, sprint speed, etc.
- Pitchers will go through a player-led warm-up routine and throw live to hitters in the scrimmage portion.
- All players will receive instruction from the coaching staff and current SLU baseball athletes during instructional hour.
 

CONTACT/REGISTRATION:
Cost: $150
