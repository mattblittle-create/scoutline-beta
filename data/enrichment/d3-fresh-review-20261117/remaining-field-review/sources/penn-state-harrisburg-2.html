Baseball


OCTOBER SHOWCASE

REGISTER

Date: Oct. 18

Time: 10:30 a.m. - 3:30 p.m.

Age: 15-17 years old (males only)

Price: $65

Overview: Penn State Harrisburg's baseball program has established itself as one of the premiere programs in the region and country. The goal of our camps is for campers and families to learn what Penn State Harrisburg is all about, while receiving instruction from head coach Ryan Bown and staff. The best way to showcase your skills and get in front of our staff is by attending our camps. The camp format will be a pro-style workout with instruction throughout the day. We will also have a Q&A session and will cover all details about our school, program and the recruiting process. An optional campus tour will be offered at the conclusion of camp. We look forward to meeting you soon. Registration closes on Oct. 8 at 11:59 p.m. No refunds will be offered.
