HORNETS NATION

At Morris College, athletics is more than competition, it’s a vital part of a well-rounded student experience. Our athletics program is designed to support both personal and academic development while promoting school pride and community engagement.

As proud members of the NAIA and the Continental Athletic Conference, our student-athletes compete at a high level while maintaining their commitment to academic success. The program offers opportunities for both men and women to grow as leaders, teammates, and individuals.

Rooted in Morris’s mission of service, faith, and excellence, our coaches and staff are dedicated to mentoring students both on and off the field. With a strong foundation in discipline and character, Hornet athletes exemplify the spirit of determination in every arena.
