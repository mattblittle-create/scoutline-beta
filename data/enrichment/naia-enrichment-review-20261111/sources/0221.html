    Emily Aabye
    Women's Volleyball
    Emily Aabye earned the Peak Retirement Planning Peak Performer of the Week! Aabye led MVNU to a 2-0 week, including a five set win over Huntington on Wednesday and a sweep of Bethel on Saturday. Against Huntington, the senior racked up 22 kills, surpassing her previous career high of 20 at Campbellsville University during her sophomore campaign. Aabye also hit a red-hot .340 from the field to go along with six digs. In the win over Bethel, Aabye recorded ten kills and three service aces.
