Team Camp

Location: Arizona Christian University - Baseball Field, 1 W. Firestorm Way, Glendale, AZ 85306
Registration Ends: 11/20/2026
 Ages: 14-22 | Starts: 4:00pm / Ends: 8:45pm

More info

Description
Our Team Camp is designed for HS Aged Club or HS Fall Teams to showcase their abilities while receiving quality instruction from ACU coaches and players.  Over the course of the entire camp players will participate in a college style evaluation with measurables taken and provided to each coach at the end of the weekend, batting practice and 2 games.  We will use our latest technology to help evaluate players with Stadium Trackman, AWRE Camera system and other forms of technology we have. We will also be providing a full campus tour of ACU which will include our brand-new locker room, baseball complex and the rest of the ACU Campus. We will also provide a full presentation about ACU Baseball and our culture as well as chapel and other information. 

General Information
Arizona Christian University Baseball Camps is for all players interested in taking their skill set to the next level whether it be hitting, fielding, or pitching. Our camps are designed to provide a next level experience as you prepare for your next step in baseball.

Equipment
Each camper must bring his own baseball equipment. Players should bring a glove, bat, helmet, cleats/spikes, running shoes or turf shoes, hat, and any other equipment they feel necessary to compete. Catchers must bring their own gear. All teams should be in uniform with Jersey's for all 3 days that match the name to the Jersey # on roster.

Check-In
Please check in 30 minutes prior (4pm) to start time on Friday for the Showcase day and night.  For all Games, please arrive 45 minutes before your game time.

Location
Arizona Christian University
Baseball Field,
1 W. Firestorm Way,
Glendale, AZ 85306

Meals
No meals are provided for this camp.  Please plan accordingly.

Refund Policy
If camp is cancelled, each team will receive a full refund (processing fees are non-refundable) or a credit to a future team camp. If a teams cancels, credit will be given for a future team camp only.

ABOUT US

ACU Baseball Camps

ACU Baseball Camps at Arizona Christian University in Phoenix, Arizona are dedicated to providing baseball camps that are instructional, informational and fun along with creating a great atmosphere to learn!

The coaching staff here at Arizona Christian University prides ourselves in teaching the game of baseball to players of all ages and ability levels. Campers will receive instruction from some of the top coaches in the country focusing on mechanics and drills to help each individual player improve. ACU Baseball Camps are dedicated to the development and growth of all campers who attend.

Our camps range in ages and we should have a program to fit your needs. If you do not see a date that works for you at this time check back to the site as we add new sessions often.

Contact Us

I signed up for camp, what do I do now?

First of all, on behalf of the Arizona Christian University camp staff we would like to say THANK YOU for signing up for our camp. You should have received a confirmation e-mail with details about the camp. There is a required waiver form which must be completed prior to the start of camp. Please bring with you at check-in on the first day of camp.

What are the ages and abilities for camp?

Prospect camps are designed for student-athletes who have at-least started high school and are interested in possibly attending ACU as a student-athlete. The instructional camps are designed for ages 7-18 and have more of an instructional focused schedule. Team Camps are for high school teams or high school age travel/club teams. Youth Team Camps will be broken down into age groups.

What should my child bring to camp?

Each player must bring his own Helmet and other baseball equipment; glove, bat, cleats/spikes, running shoes or turf shoes, hat, and any other equipment they feel necessary to compete. Catchers must bring their own gear. Players should bring their own lunch unless specified that lunch is included in camp. We will provide water, however bringing a water bottle of your own is highly suggested as well.

Where are the camps located?

Apollo High School 8045 N 47th Ave, Glendale, AZ 85302

Can parents stay and watch camp?

Yes, parents are always welcome to stay and watch camp. We encourage and want a family atmosphere.

Do you offer refunds for canceled enrollment?

If camp is cancelled, each camper will receive a full refund or a credit to a future camp. If a camper cancels, credit will be given for a future camp only.
