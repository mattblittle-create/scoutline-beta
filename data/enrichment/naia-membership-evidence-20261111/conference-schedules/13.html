    Mission

    The mission of the HBCU Athletic Conference is to support our member student-athletes in fulfilling their potential through HBCU culture and experiences.

    Vision

    The HBCU Athletic Conference is committed to amplifying the excellence of HBCU culture, championships, and community through partnerships that provide financial sustainability for its members and a first-class championship experience for its student-athletes.
