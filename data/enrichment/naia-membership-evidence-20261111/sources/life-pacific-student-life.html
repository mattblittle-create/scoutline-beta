Residence Life

It is often in the ordinary, messy, and mundane moments of living in community that we find and build life-long friendships. From where (and what) you eat to who you share a room with, this is where LPU truly becomes home.

Athletics

The Warrior spirit is untouchable. We have 6 NAIA intercollegiate teams, including both men’s and women’s sports, from basketball to soccer to beach volleyball. Show your pride, Warriors!

Global Life

Life Pacific University values experiential and cross-cultural learning. We are committed to providing opportunities for our students to participate in off-campus study programs.

Diversity & Reconciliation

Life Pacific University is committed to creating a healthy environment for living and learning for all members of our community. We celebrate diversity as an expression of God’s creativity and aim to build a diverse, Christ-centered community. Let’s make LPU safe and welcoming.

Student Employment

LPU Students comprise an integral part of the institutions workforce. Applications submitted are automatically routed to the Human Resources and hiring departments. Questions may be directed to Human Resources at hbonadie@lifepacific.edu. Applications must be completed in one sitting, so be sure to note any supplemental attachments required and allow adequate time to complete before beginning an application. 

Search Jobs

Career Placement

Utilizing Handshake and networking, we support the graduate to career transition, working hard to help place our students in the fields they desire. By posting a job with us, we give positions visibility, extending to all LPU students (East and West campuses, undergrad, graduate and online) as well as our alumni and ministry leaders within the Foursquare Denomination.
