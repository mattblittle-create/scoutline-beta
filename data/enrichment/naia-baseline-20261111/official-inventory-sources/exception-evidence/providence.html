School Closure Information for Providence Christian College - Pasadena, CA

The Office of Student Assistance and Relief (OSAR) has been notified that Providence Christian College, located at 464 E. Walnut Street, Pasadena, CA91101, closed on June 30 with a last day of instruction reported on May 15, 2026.

Prior to this closure, Providence Christian College was operating under a limited exemption.

The following information is provided for former students:

Request Your Records

We encourage students to request transcripts, enrollment agreements, student ledgers, and other important documents as soon as possible after a school closure. Use the contact information below to obtain your records:
