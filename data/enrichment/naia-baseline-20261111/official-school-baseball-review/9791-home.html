Athletics Day of Giving

June 11, 2026 - August 4, 2026

Join us, for the third annual Grace Athletics Day of Giving!

We are calling on our Lancer Nation, far and wide, to rally behind our players and teams. The Lancers have won 53 Crossroads League championships and 19 national championships, and our teams just keep getting better! With nearly 400 student-athletes on our 19 varsity teams, the need for more fans and supporters continues to grow.

How you can rally behind our Lancers:

  • Pray for our coaches and student-athletes, as they strive to know Christ and make Him known—on and off the field/court.
  • Share about Grace Athletics Day of Giving and celebrate our wins on social media.
  • Give and join. Partner with us in training our student-athletes. Complete the online form to give today! The first $10,000 raised will be matched 1:1 by an anonymous donor.

 Donations on Grace Athletics Day of Giving will directly support our student-athletes as they travel, compete, and become champions for Christ. Partner with us today!
