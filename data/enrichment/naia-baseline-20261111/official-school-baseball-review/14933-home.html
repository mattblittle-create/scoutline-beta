Welcome Our New Head Women’s Volleyball Coach, Silvino Bueno

Please join us in welcoming Silvino Bueno as our new Head Women’s Volleyball Coach. With coaching experience in Brazil and the United States, Silvino brings a passion for developing student-athletes both on and off the court. His leadership is rooted in discipline, integrity, and long-term growth, making him a valuable addition to our program and community.

Champion Christian College Accepted into the NAIA

Champion Christian College proudly announces its acceptance into the National Association of Intercollegiate Athletics (NAIA), marking a major milestone in the continued growth and advancement of Tiger Athletics. This transition represents a strategic step forward—expanding competitive opportunities while reinforcing the college’s commitment to academic excellence, character development, and Christ-centered values.
