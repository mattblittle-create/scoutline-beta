Fisher Falcons 

At Fisher College, being a student-athlete means more than pursuing a degree - it’s about gaining real-world experience, building practical skills, and preparing for success in both the classroom and the workplace.

Our Falcons are part of a global community that values diversity, collaboration, and ambition, and student-athletes play a vital role in shaping the spirit and success of our programs. 

Fisher College offers eight athletic programs that provide opportunities for competition, leadership, and personal growth. With a strong commitment to excellence, our programs are designed to help student-athletes balance their academic and athletic goals while developing the skills necessary to succeed in all aspects of life. 

We are dedicated to fostering an empowering environment where athletic passion and academic ambitions intersect. With opportunities for athletic achievement, personal development, and community, Fisher College provides a platform to compete at a high level while receiving a world-class education. 

Our mission is to support you as a student, athlete, and individual, equipping you with the tools and experiences to thrive after graduation and in every future endeavor. At Fisher College, your journey extends far beyond the game. 

In my experience as a baseball player and student at Fisher, it is a good place to meet people who, at this point in your life, become like a second family. The treatment from the teachers and coaches allow you to feel cared for and supported.

Fraser Field 

Located in Lynn, Massachusetts, Fraser Field has been the home of Fisher College Baseball since the 2011 season. With a seating capacity of 3,800, this historic ballpark offers a unique and inspiring venue for our student-athletes to compete. Originally built in 1940 as part of President Franklin D. Roosevelt’s New Deal and the Works Progress Administration, Fraser Field has a rich history as the former home to several minor league affiliates, including the Lynn Red Sox (an affiliate of the Boston Red Sox), the Lynn Sailors (affiliated with the Seattle Mariners), and the Lynn Pirates (affiliated with the Pittsburgh Pirates). 

Today, Fraser Field combines tradition, history, and modern athletic opportunity, providing the perfect setting for Fisher College’s baseball program and its dedication to excellence both on and off the field. 

Daly Field 

Located in Brighton, Massachusetts, Daly Field is the home of the Fisher College Softball program. Opened in the summer of 2016, this state-of-the-art facility features a synthetic turf playing surface with a clay pitching circle, offering the perfect blend of durability and performance. Equipped with lights and a scoreboard, Daly Field provides an exceptional game-day experience and has served as the primary venue for Fisher Softball since 2021. 

Pappas Gymnasium 

Located in Brookline, Massachusetts, Pappas Gymnasium serves as the home for the Fisher College Basketball and Volleyball programs. The facility accommodates up to 100 spectators and features a fully equipped weight room with a view overlooking the court that is available for use by our student-athletes.   

Pappas Gymnasium holds local historical significance, as it was the former practice facility for the Boston Celtics during the 1980s. The court remains remarkably unchanged, offering a nostalgic connection to NBA legends like Larry Bird and Red Auerbach. Fisher College began calling Pappas Gymnasium home starting with the 2021-22 season, creating a modern and inspiring space for competition, training, and community. 

Hormel Stadium 

Hormel Stadium, located in Medford, Massachusetts, is the home facility for the Fisher College Men’s and Women’s Soccer teams. With a seating capacity of 2,500, it’s one of the premier venues in the area for collegiate soccer events. Fisher has called Hormel Stadium home since the 2013 season. Recently renovated, the stadium features modern amenities such as stadium-style seating, lighting for evening games, a press box, and indoor restroom facilities, providing an exceptional experience for both athletes and fans. Hormel Stadium offers a top-tier environment for competition, training, and community spirit.
