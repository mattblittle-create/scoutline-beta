Unleash Your Inner Champion

As a Cougar, you will discover your true meaning, both as an athlete and as a student. Our award winning coaches will mentor you as you’ll face some of the best competition that the NAIA has to offer. On campus, you’ll be challenged academically and grow in your relationship with Jesus Christ. Through the relationships you build and encounters you’ll face, you’ll be ready for the next game — and the rest of your life.

Athletic Facilities Highlight

CH4 Soccer and Lacrosse Stadium: Features a turf field, lights, scoreboard, stadium seating, patio, concessions, locker rooms, and community fields.

Ariel Arena: MVNU’s state-of-the-art athletics facility features a full-sized athletic court, Athletic Training Center, stadium seating for 2,650, cardio fitness and weight rooms.

Ramser Tennis Courts: Located on the west side of Martinsburg Road and include LED lighting for night play.

Energy Fieldhouse: A shared/collaborative space with Mount Vernon High School featuring a 6 lane indoor competition track.

Cougar Baseball Field: Synthetic turf infield and natural grass outfield, playable in almost all weather conditions, press box, electronic scoreboard and bleacher seating.

Cougar Softball Field: Playable in almost all weather conditions, press box and electronic scoreboard.

Prince Student Union and Donoho Recreation Center: The Donoho Recreation Center has two gyms that are used for practice and intramurals.
