Athletics

Athletics are a central part of the college experience — and at Holy Cross, there is plenty of opportunity to show your Crusader spirit.

Whether you're looking to be a student-athlete, or you'd like to cheer on your fellow Crusaders, there's always a way to connect with our teams. As a student, attending all regular season home sporting events is free!

#GoCrossGo

Holy Cross student-athletes strive for comprehensive excellence — in competition, the classroom and the community.

  • 27 Division I athletic teams
  • 1 in 4 Students compete in varsity athletics
  • 97% Graduation rate among student-athletes
  • 87 Conference regular season and tournament championships, along with two national championships

Proud Member of the

Patriot League, Atlantic Hockey America and Hockey East

On game days, the passion for purple from fans and alumni sparks one of the best atmospheres within the Patriot League, Atlantic Hockey America and Hockey East. Holy Cross is a founding member of the Patriot League, recognized for placing as much emphasis on academic and leadership success as athletic achievement. In fact, Holy Cross itself is renowned for its consistently high student-athlete graduation rates — among the best in the nation.
