Bobcat Baseball Camps

New Family Enrollment    Parent Dashboard

Head baseball coach Cody Pentecost is proud to announce the College of the Ozarks baseball team will be hosting Bobcat Baseball Camps this summer and fall. These camps are for all interested high school players from grades 10-12. Each player will be evaluated by Coach Pentecost and his staff with input from guest instructors.

These camps will be capped at 60 participants and will likely fill up quickly.

Bobcat Baseball Prospect Camps

  • DATES: October 3 and 17
  • Grades: 10-12
  • TIME: Check-in 8:30-9:15 a.m.
  • CAMP: 9:30-4:00 p.m. (Lunch break at noon - LUNCH IS NOT PROVIDED)
  • COST: $100 (Cost includes T-Shirt) 

    This camp will be capped at 60 participants and will likely fill up quickly.
