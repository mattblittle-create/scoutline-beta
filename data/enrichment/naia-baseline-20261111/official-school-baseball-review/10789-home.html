    Small Campus. Big Opportunity.

    At IU Columbus, you’re not just another number. Our small campus environment provides a personalized experience both inside and outside the classroom, where professors know you by name and are there to help you succeed throughout your journey. 

    Our challenging degree programs are designed to prepare you for the hottest jobs in Indiana and beyond, so you will have the skills, knowledge, and confidence needed to be successful in the career you want.
