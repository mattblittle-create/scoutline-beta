Welcome to Royals Athletics at Johnson University — where faith fuels competition and character defines success.

Here, relentless drive meets deeper purpose as our coaches develop athletes who compete with integrity, lead with humility, and pursue excellence for the glory of God. On our fields, courts, and tracks, good athletes become great ones—on and off the field.

Play for JU Royals

Put on the crown. Compete with purpose. When you become a Royal, you join a tradition of athletes who play with heart, hustle, and honor. We field competitive teams across 10 sports, each with dedicated coaches committed to your growth as an athlete and as a person. This is your chance to compete at the next level while earning a degree that matters.

Head Coach Dayna Carter

Dayna Carter joined Johnson University as the Assistant Athletic Director and Head Softball Coach in 2021. She previously coached softball at the University of Tennessee- Knoxville as well as a local high school.

Head Coach Dave Serrano

Dave Serrano started as Head Baseball Coach for Johnson in 2023. He has coached for many baseball teams over the years including, the University of Tennessee – Knoxville, Cal State Northridge, as well as the USA Collegiate team.
Check out the full bio

Head Coach Bryon Lawhon

Bryon Lawhon started as Head Women’s Basketball Coach in 2023. He previously coached at Bryan College where he won AAC Coach of the year, had an undefeated season, and won AAC Regular Season Champion (2021-22).
Check out the full bio

Head Coach Logan Sharp

Logan Sharp joined the royals in 2023 and exceeded expectations by leading his team to 4th overall in the AAC standings after being picked 11th in the preseason poll. Before joining the Royals, he spent two successful seasons as the Head Coach at Eastern Nazarene College.
Check out the full bio

Head Coach Todd Halliburton

Todd Halliburton joined the coaching staff at Johnson University in 2019. He had just gotten back into coaching as a volunteer coach at a local high school. Todd has been a coach and a pastor for a long time but one thing that people may not know about him is that he has been in more than 60 movies, television shows, commercials and music videos.
Check out the full bio

Head Coach Isaac Morris

Isaac Morris joined the royals in 2023 as Head Men’s Soccer Coach. He had previously been the assistant coach for the Johnson soccer team for two years and is a former Royals player (2015-19).
Check out the full bio

Head Coach Brody Blalock

Brody Blalock joined Johnson as a graduate assistant for the softball team in January of 2025. In April, he accepted a position as the head cross country coach and was tasked with rebuilding the program.
Check out the full bio

Head Coach Seth Hale

Seth Hale founded the Johnson University Tennis program in 2014 and has served as the head coach ever since.
Check out the full bio

Head Coach Jace Devault

Jace Devault is entering his second season as the Head Men's Golf Coach at Johnson University, where he is leading the exciting relaunch of the Royals’ golf program.
Check out the full bio

Head Coach Cece Blanchard

Cece Blanchard is the head volleyball coach at Johnson University, bringing experience from Lee University and University of Alabama in Huntsville. A former student-athlete at Union University, she is focused on developing student-athletes through relationships, faith, and competitive excellence.

Head Coach Nate Shadoan

Nate Shadoan is the inaugural head men’s volleyball coach at Johnson University, bringing experience from Emmanuel University and assistant roles at Bryan College, Lee University, and Union University. An Appalachian Athletic Conference Assistant Coach of the Year, he is focused on building a competitive program centered on development, accountability, and culture.

Head Coach Eboni McCarthy

Eboni McCarthy, a native of Madison, Alabama, is a lifelong swimmer who competed at the University of Georgia, reaching the SEC and NCAA Championships. She discovered her passion for coaching after graduation and returned to Alabama to coach with her former club team. McCarthy now leads Johnson’s swim program with a focus on developing student-athletes in and out of the pool.

FAQs

Answers to the most popular questions about JU Athletics.

  • The best way to get recruited at Johnson University is to contact the coach and talk to the Admissions team. The coaches get hundreds of emails and texts about playing, so the best way is to be persistent and reach out multiple times!

  • Yes, we offer athletic scholarships! However, we do not stack scholarships for student athletes, which means you are either on athletic or academic scholarship, but you cannot be on both. Aid is awarded based on coach’s discretion, and every team has a limited pool of aid to give out.

  • Being a college student-athlete is essentially a full-time job, but if you stay on top of everything and are committed, then you will find being a part of the team is helpful towards your academic endeavors! Academics come first, and student-athletes need to recognize classwork and travel times will overlap, but deadlines are expected to be met.

  • Johnson University Athletics is a member of the National Association of Intercollegiate Athletics (NAIA) and belong to the Appalachian Athletic Conference (AAC).

  • Tryouts are on an individual basis and are at coaches’ discretion. If you are interested, please contact the coaches as we do not do traditional large group tryouts.
