Athletics

You are our home court advantage

Whether you’re competing, cheering, playing in the pep band, or howling from the sidelines – Red Wolf pride is contagious!

Tailgate with Rufus, our beloved mascot, and carry that spirit into the state of the art Student Events Center where Red Wolves home basketball games and volleyball matches are played. Students never pay admission to home games and, with themed game nights and half-time contests, you’ll load up with free Red Wolves gear!
